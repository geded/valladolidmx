- Business short links: `/<categoría>/<empresa>` (`src/routes/$categoria.$empresa.tsx` over `short-links*`) only 301-redirects published businesses to their canonical territorial page, accepting the category slug in plural or Spanish singular; unknown pairs return 404. Why: promotional addresses without a second public identity or changing the Navigation Blueprint.
- Return to origin: anything that sends a visitor to `/auth` calls `rememberAuthReturnPath(path, label)` (session plus a 2-hour device copy so email confirmation in another tab returns), and anything that opens `/cuenta/checkout/$orderId` calls `rememberCheckoutOrigin`, which the checkout shows as «Volver a …» and clears the saved carta order once paid. Why: travelers never lose the business page or their selection when signing in or paying.
