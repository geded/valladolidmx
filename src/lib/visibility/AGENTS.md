# Visibility rules

- Listing order: full public listings (business families via `buildPublicListing`, experiences via `experience-public-reads.server.ts`) receive organic alphabetical rows and place paid packages with `arrangeListing` in `commercial-slots.ts` — first card of every block of 4, one commercial slot per business, pinned spotlights first, weighted daily rotation, nothing hidden; only the slotted card is marked `commercialPlacement: "contratado"` («Recomendado»). Why: the approved 2026-09-18 visibility plan bounds paid exposure in every listing instead of an unbounded boost sort.
- Profile quality: the Portal meter «Tu ficha para Alux», Alux reading/ranking quality and the monthly report all derive from the pure `profile-completeness.ts` over the single read `business_profile_signals` (published rows, own businesses, or staff). Quality stays below question match and paid weight; ties between free businesses rotate daily with `fairTurnKey`, never alphabetically, in Alux and in `arrangeBusinessListing` (photo first). Why: the free plan must earn recommendations by completing the profile without overriding Google-style paid placement.
- Monthly report: `/api/public/hooks/business-monthly-report` runs daily; `monthly-report.ts` decides the send day and the previous Mérida month, `list_business_monthly_report_rows` (service role) supplies counts for businesses with an owner, and `business_monthly_reports` (unique per business and month) is claimed before email (`sendVisibilityEmail`) and WhatsApp (platform `vmx-*` line only). Why: one idempotent sender, no traveler data, and the send day changes in one constant.
- Destination sections: `getDestinationRelated` sends at most `DESTINATION_SECTION_SAMPLE` businesses per section, chosen by `pickDestinationSection` (`src/lib/destinations/destination-rail-order.ts`) over `arrangeListing` (spotlights, paid packages, photo-first fair turn); editor pins in «otras» stay first. Why: the page shows a sample, full lists live in each family listing, and the old alphabetical scan ignored packages.
