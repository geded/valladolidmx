# Taxi Alux rules

- Payment exports neutralize spreadsheet formula prefixes before CSV quoting in `taxiLedgerCsv`. Why: traveler and driver text must remain data when administrators open the ledger.

- Taxi Alux uses `platform_settings` key `taxi_alux.settings` plus `taxi_drivers`, `taxi_rides`, `taxi_ride_events` and private `taxi_driver_invitations`; public quotes, token-only applications and every write go through RPCs, while the admin, traveler and driver surfaces use the existing PublicShell/Workspace Engine. Why: one fare/availability and admin-approval authority, no parallel transport/payment model, and existing rides remain operable when new requests are switched off.
- Uber-style flow: quotes auto-run once pickup and destination exist (place search via `searchTaxiPlaces` over published coordinates), ETAs come only from `taxiEtaMinutes`/`taxiEtaHeadline` in `rules.ts`, ratings via `taxi_rate_ride`, and live sharing only through the traveler's `taxi_share_ride` token read by anon `taxi_shared_ride` (no PII, expires 2 h after the ride). Why: one ETA/rating authority and a share link that never exposes accounts.
- Pickup zones: rides may start only inside admin-marked departure destinations (`taxi_alux.pickup_zones`, default Valladolid, radius km) checked by `taxi_pickup_allowed` inside `taxi_request_ride` and mirrored by `taxiPickupZoneFor`; destinations are free anywhere. «Pedir taxi aquí» (`TaxiToPlaceButton`) is always shown on places with coordinates; the taxi screen explains when pickup is outside a zone. Why: the network grows destination by destination without blocking trips out of Valladolid.
- Fleet pricing: vehicle class (seats + multiplier over the compact fare), pickup wait, stops and hourly round trips live under `fleet` in `taxi_alux.settings` and are priced only by SQL `taxi_quote`; drivers see/accept only rides whose class seats fit their vehicle; wait is added by trigger when the ride starts. Why: Uber/DiDi-style pricing by vehicle, never per person, with one server-side price authority.
- Taxi fares use Google road distance (signed-in quotes and every ride request, cached 30 min, quota-bounded) passed as `_road_km`, clamped in SQL to 1x-4x straight distance; visitors without an account get one road quote per device (IP-bounded `taxi.roadRoute.anon`), then must sign up to quote again; never show a straight-line price. Why: real road km without an open paid proxy or client-forged prices.
